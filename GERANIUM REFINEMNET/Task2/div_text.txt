How the Division forwards a complaint
A complaint received by the Division against an authorized company is entered in the Division's register, given a reference in the form shown above, and forwarded to the company by electronic transmission to the address the company has designated for the purpose. The company is required to acknowledge receipt within five working days and to enter the complaint in its own register on receipt. The Division retains the transmission record and the acknowledgment for every complaint it forwards, and both are on this file for each of the complaints listed above.
The Division's register is the record of what was forwarded. Where a company's register and the Division's register disagree about whether a complaint was forwarded, the question is settled by the Division's transmission record and not by the company's recollection.
The order made on the previous examination
The Commissioner's order adopting the previous report required the company to file a corrective action plan for each standard found, to report quarterly on its progress for four quarters, and to submit to a further examination at the Commissioner's discretion. The company filed a plan for each of the two standards and made all four quarterly reports. The Division took no further action at the time and has formed no view, before this examination, on whether either plan worked.
What this file does not contain
The Division holds no record of any complaint about this company from a source other than a consumer complaint forwarded as described above. It holds no examination of the company's underwriting, rating or policyholder service, none having been conducted. Nothing on this file bears on the accuracy of the dates in the company's own claim system, which the Division has not tested and does not warrant.
