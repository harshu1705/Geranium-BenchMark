Restitution
Three total loss settlements were paid without the sales tax, the title and registration fee, or both. The company pays the difference with interest at ten percent simple from the day it paid to the day of this report, on a basis of three hundred and sixty five days, under 6.4.
A settlement on one of these files is the actual cash value, plus sales tax at seven and one half percent of that value, plus the title and registration fee of 42.50. Against that, CM-25-0207 was issued 14,382.00, which is the actual cash value and nothing else. CM-25-0498 was issued 10,475.88, which is short the fee alone. CM-25-0803 was issued 21,206.50, which is short the tax. The other three settlements were issued in full and are not in exception.

What this examination does not settle
The dates tested are the dates the company's own claim system holds. The Division has not tested that system and the report does not warrant that the dates in it are the dates on which the acts recorded actually happened. Where a date in the system is wrong the exception count on the standard it bears on would move with it.
The examination reaches claims handling and nothing else. No underwriting, rating or policyholder service practice of the company has been examined, and nothing in this report should be read as a finding that any of them complies with anything.
Whether the company received notice of any complaint the Division did not forward is outside this examination. Standard 5.1 measures the register against what the Division forwarded, and the Division's record of that is what governs under 5.2.
The company produced the sample itself and represented the production complete. The Division has not audited it. A file the company did not produce would not appear in any denominator or in any exception above.
