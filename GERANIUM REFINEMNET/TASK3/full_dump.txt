[P11] Decision
[P12] The review covered 32 closed-paid repair claims totaling $232,699.69. Fourteen claims had at least one monetary or sequencing exception, including six High and eight Moderate files. Monetary exposure is $4,500.75, or 1.9% of final repair paid. Unsupported non-labor approvals account for $4,243.25; unauthorized labor-rate variance adds $257.50.
[P13] A broad rate response is not supported. Unauthorized rate variance appears on 9 of 32 claims (28.1%) and at three facilities. The standard requires at least 30 percent of reviewed claims, four facilities and consistent market support. The notes instead show isolated approvals and one heavily concentrated facility pattern. F-211 accounts for $4,414.75, or 98.1% of all monetary exposure.

[P17] Review basis
[P18] Every claim was matched across the closed-claim, supplement and timeline extracts. Labor lines were tested against the county and operation ceiling effective on the approval date. Valid aluminum and remote premiums were retained only where facility eligibility and code requirements were satisfied. Non-labor approvals above $150 were tested for evidence by closure, and original approvals were tested against inspection order and FAST-8 requirements. Dollar exposure includes only excess labor above the permitted rate and unsupported non-labor approvals; sequencing-only events add no dollars.
[P19] Valid special handling was left in place. ALUM-08 was supported on AZR-26-0706, AZR-26-0718 and AZR-26-0732. REMOTE-06 was supported on AZR-26-0712 and AZR-26-0728. EMERG-2 was valid on AZR-26-0711 and AZR-26-0728, while FAST-8 was valid on AZR-26-0703, AZR-26-0711 and AZR-26-0728. None of these events was counted as leakage.

[P42] High-file review: Claim Management should review the six High files by September 16, 2026. Record the recovery assessment, documentation follow-up and coding disposition for each file before closing the review. Exposure totals $4,414.75; it is not an established recovery entitlement.
[P43] Workflow edit: Claims Systems should implement and test the approval block by September 16, 2026. For non-labor approvals above $150, require received evidence or a valid EMERG-2 note. Before release, verify that supported approvals proceed, documented emergencies proceed, and unsupported approvals are blocked; evidence added later must not erase the original sequencing event.
[P44] Thirty-day resample: Regional Appraisal Quality owns a repeat test of the first 30 closed-paid files after the control period. Complete it within 30 calendar days after that period ends and verify claim count, rate incidence, facility count, market support and exposure reconciliation. Open a rate study only if the resample reaches the standard's 30-percent, four-facility and market-support thresholds.
