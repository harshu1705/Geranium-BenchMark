The 60% figure appears only in the summary note and cannot be independently verified from the listed components without a weighting formula. This report does not invent such a formula. Regardless of the aggregation method, however, the reported environment remains materially below the Policy 4.5.c requirement, and the SLA results carry a qualification: they are not representative of full production load.
