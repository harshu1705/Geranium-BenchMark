Tri-County Community Bank — Q4 2025 ALLL Reserve Adequacy Memorandum
Prepared by Credit Risk Division | Review Date: January 23, 2026
1. Executive Summary
This memorandum tests the adequacy of Tri-County Community Bank's Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses (ALLL) for the Q4 2025 commercial loan portfolio under the methodology set out in the ALLL/CECL Reserve Policy. The portfolio comprises 29 active commercial loans with an aggregate outstanding balance of $134,656,509.
The total reserve required under this methodology is $1,661,149, combining $1,320,509 in pooled reserves across Risk Rating pools 1 through 5 and a $340,640 specific reserve for the one individually evaluated loan, LN-008 (Palmetto Print & Packaging Inc.), which is collateral-dependent following its Chapter 11 filing. Against a currently booked ALLL balance of $1,250,000, the portfolio carries a reserve shortfall of $411,149. This shortfall exceeds the 10% adequacy tolerance band in Policy Section 6, and an additional provision of $411,149 should be recognized in the current quarter.
2. Portfolio Segmentation
All 29 loans were segmented by Internal Risk Rating as recorded in Loan_Register_Q4_2025.xlsx. Ratings 1 through 5 are pooled; Rating 6 is individually evaluated per Policy Section 4 and excluded from the pooled segmentation below.
Segment counts and balances were independently re-tallied from the Loan Register rather than taken from any pre-existing summary; the six segments sum to the full portfolio balance of $134,656,509 and the full loan count of 29, confirming no loan was omitted or double-counted.
3. Pooled Reserve Calculation
Base historical loss rates were taken from ALLL_Loss_Rate_and_Reserve_Data.xlsx. Before applying them, each pool was checked against the Covenant Compliance Workbook, Borrower Financial Statements, and Appraisal and Collateral Summary to determine whether it contains an active covenant breach requiring a Policy Section 5 Q-Factor add-on of 25 basis points. Breach status was independently recomputed rather than accepted from the workbook's pre-calculated fields: DSCR was recomputed as Net Operating Income divided by Total Annual Debt Service, LTV as Outstanding Balance divided by Appraised Value, and Current Ratio as Current Assets divided by Current Liabilities. Pool 3 contains three breaching loans (LN-004 DSCR 1.14x vs. 1.20x, LN-006 Current Ratio 1.03x vs. 1.20x, LN-010 LTV 85.2% vs. 75%); Pool 4 contains one breaching loan (LN-001, DSCR 1.07x vs. 1.20x); Pool 5 contains one breaching loan (LN-002, LTV 87.9% vs. 75% and Current Ratio 0.94x vs. 1.00x). Pools 1 and 2 contain no loans with an active covenant breach and receive no Q-Factor add-on.
Total pooled reserve across all five pools: $1,320,509.
4. Individually Evaluated Loan Analysis
LN-008 (Palmetto Print & Packaging Inc.) carries an Internal Risk Rating of 6 and is 97 days past due, meeting both individual-evaluation triggers under Policy Section 4. The loan is collateral-dependent: the borrower filed Chapter 11 on December 9, 2025, and repayment is expected to be provided through liquidation of the manufacturing equipment pledged as collateral.
This specific reserve requirement is corroborated by Exception EXC-010 in the Exception Tracking Log, which independently records a loss reserve as active for this loan, and by the Appraisal and Collateral Summary note referencing the Chapter 11 filing. No other loan in the portfolio is rated 6 or 90+ days past due; no other loan requires individual evaluation.
5. Covenant Breach and Watch List Cross-Reference
Five loans carry an active covenant breach as of the review date: LN-001, LN-002, LN-004, LN-006, and LN-010 (LN-008 is excluded from this pool-level analysis because it is individually evaluated in Section 4, not pooled). LN-001, LN-002, and LN-008 additionally carry a Watch List designation under the Loan Portfolio Review Policy (Internal Risk Rating of 4 or higher). The Watch List designation itself does not directly change the pooled reserve calculation — the Risk Rating pool already reflects elevated risk through its base historical loss rate — but the presence of a breach within a Watch List loan's pool is what triggers the Q-Factor add-on documented in Section 3. LN-004, LN-006, and LN-010 are not on the Watch List (their Internal Risk Ratings are 3, below the Rating ≥ 4 threshold), but their breaches still trigger the Pool 3 Q-Factor because the Q-Factor is a pool-level, not loan-level, adjustment.
6. Reserve Adequacy Conclusion
The $411,149 shortfall represents 32.9% of the currently booked balance, well outside the ±10% adequacy tolerance band in Policy Section 6. The reserve is therefore inadequate, and an additional provision of $411,149 should be recognized in the current quarter and reported to the Board Audit Committee. The shortfall is driven primarily by the Rating 5 pool (LN-002, $703,452 of required reserve on a single loan) and the LN-008 specific reserve ($340,640), both of which reflect deterioration — a payment demand and dual covenant breach on LN-002, and a Chapter 11 filing with declining collateral value on LN-008 — that the currently booked balance does not appear to fully capture.
7. Policy/Source Reconciliation
Historical loss rates, the disposition cost assumption, the Q-Factor rate, and the currently booked ALLL balance were taken directly from ALLL_Loss_Rate_and_Reserve_Data.xlsx as pre-calculated policy inputs; no source conflicts were found among these figures. Risk Rating pool composition was independently re-tallied from Loan_Register_Q4_2025.xlsx rather than assumed, and the six segments were confirmed to sum to the full 29-loan, $134,656,509 portfolio.
Covenant breach status feeding the Q-Factor determination was independently recomputed rather than read from the Covenant Compliance Workbook's pre-calculated actual-vs-threshold fields: DSCR was recomputed from Borrower_Financial_Statements_Summary.xlsx (Net Operating Income ÷ Total Annual Debt Service), LTV from the Loan Register outstanding balance divided by the Appraisal and Collateral Summary appraised value, and Current Ratio from Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities in the financial statements file. All recomputed values matched the workbook's pre-calculated figures within normal rounding tolerance (±0.01x or ±0.1 percentage point), and no conflicts were identified between the pre-calculated workbook values and the independently derived calculations.
The LN-008 specific reserve calculation used the most recent appraised collateral value on file (the December 2025 collateral inspection in the Appraisal and Collateral Summary, $830,000) rather than an older or origination-date value, consistent with Policy Section 4's requirement to use the most recent appraisal regardless of loan origination date. This treatment is corroborated by Exception EXC-010, which independently documents an active loss reserve for this loan in the Exception Tracking Log.
